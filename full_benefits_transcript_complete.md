## User Query 1
Find alternatives to TANF that one person can apply for 

Cannot be snap, wic

## Assistant Response 1
If you exclude TANF, SNAP, and WIC, the remaining assistance options in the U.S. fall into a few categories: disability-based aid, income-based cash equivalents, housing support, healthcare coverage, and utility/energy assistance. What you can apply for depends heavily on income, employment status, disability status, and household composition.

In Texas (including Austin/Travis County), the main alternatives are:

### Disability-based cash or income support
- Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
  Monthly federal cash assistance for aged, blind, or disabled individuals with very low income and resources. This is the closest non-TANF “cash” program for individuals.

- Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)
  For individuals with sufficient prior work history who have a qualifying disability. Not means-tested like TANF.

### Healthcare coverage (often overlooked as “support”)
- Medicaid
  In Texas, eligibility is more restrictive than many states, but it covers some low-income adults.

- Medicare (if disabled long-term or over 65)

### Housing assistance
- Housing Choice Voucher Program (Section 8)
  Long waiting lists, but it subsidizes rent.

- Public Housing via local housing authorities

### Utility and emergency financial assistance
- LIHEAP
  Helps pay electricity, gas, cooling costs.

- Texas Utility Help

### Employment-based support
- Unemployment Insurance (UI)

- Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)

### Local Austin / Travis County assistance
- Salvation Army Austin
- Caritas of Austin
- SAFE Alliance
