# Free Mobile Plans Still Available in 2026

## Overview

There are very few mobile plans that are genuinely “free forever” with no rotating promo period. Most fall into three categories:

1. U.S. government-subsidized Lifeline plans  
2. Ad-supported/free-tier experimental carriers  
3. VOIP/data-only app services

As of 2026, the only consistently available nationwide options are Lifeline-based carriers for eligible low-income users.

Current options:

- Assurance Wireless — Free Lifeline plan with unlimited talk/text and around 10GB high-speed data in many states. Uses the T-Mobile network.
- SafetyNet Wireless — Free Lifeline service with no monthly bill or contract. Availability depends on state.
- Gen Mobile Lifeline — Free Lifeline plan in many states, often including talk/text/data and sometimes a free phone.
- Thrive Mobile Lifeline — $0/month Lifeline option with limited data and unlimited talk/text.
- SafeLink Wireless — Long-running Lifeline provider, typically free if eligible.
- Q Link Wireless — Free Lifeline-based service in many areas with talk/text/data.
- TruConnect — Free Lifeline service in supported states.
- AirTalk Wireless — Lifeline provider known for occasionally offering free phones plus free monthly service.

Important limitations:

- Lifeline is income/benefit-qualified only.
- One benefit per household.
- You generally must use the service at least once every 30 days to keep it active.
- ACP (“Affordable Connectivity Program”) largely ended, so older “unlimited free everything” plans became more restricted.

# Additional Notes

## Lifeline Eligibility

All carriers listed above are primarily tied to the federal Lifeline program. Eligibility is generally determined through either:

- Participation in qualifying assistance programs
- Income qualification at or below 135% of the Federal Poverty Level

## Household Restrictions

Most Lifeline-supported plans enforce the following:

- One Lifeline benefit per household
- Periodic recertification may be required
- Inactivity can result in cancellation

## Network Variability

Several providers switch between underlying networks depending on region, SIM inventory, or agreements. Actual network access may vary by:

- State
- ZIP code
- Device compatibility
- SIM type
- eSIM support status

## Device Policies

Some providers emphasize:

- Free government-issued Android phones
- Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
- eSIM activation
- Physical SIM-only activation

Availability changes frequently depending on inventory and state programs.
