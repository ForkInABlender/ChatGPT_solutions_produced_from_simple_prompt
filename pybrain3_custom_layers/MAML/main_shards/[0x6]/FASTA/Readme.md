# Why Fasta Data?


This data is essential for mimicking the human head. The data is mostly focused on the parts of the brain that make use of them.
 This will be used for neurochemical and biochemical modeling with MRI data. The point is to train it to mimick the electrochemical properties as 
well as the electrical interjunctions. From that point, it is connecting the activation functional states of the two trees.

The next step is to walk the 2 parts, their weights, and their electrical spike upon uptake of each during normal processing. The next after that
 is calculating input and output beyond NLP LLM modelling. Remember, the brain must operate asynchronously even in a mirror of it.
